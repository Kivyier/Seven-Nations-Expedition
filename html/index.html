Frequently asked questions

Answers based on the package information we have so far. Anything not yet decided by the agency is marked as "to be confirmed."

What does the price of a package cover?

Each package shows a base price per person for the listed length of stay. The exact inclusions, such as meals, transport, and accommodation type, will be confirmed by the agency when you send an inquiry.

Is the price per person or per group?

Prices are shown per person unless a package says otherwise. Group rates, if available, will be confirmed by the agency.

How long are the tours?

Each package lists its length, such as 3D2N or 4D3N, on its card and details page.

What is the package code for?

The package code, such as MOND-01, is a short reference you can quote when sending an inquiry or asking about a specific package.

How do I book a package?

Use the booking form on the package's details page, or send a travel inquiry and the agency will follow up.

Can I change the featured stops or the schedule?

To be confirmed by the agency. Send an inquiry with your preferred changes and they will let you know what's possible.

What is the cancellation or refund policy?

To be confirmed by the agency.
