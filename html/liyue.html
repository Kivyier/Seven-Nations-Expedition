Featured stops

  • Liyue Harbor

    A subarea and a main city located in Sea of Clouds, Liyue.Before the Archon War, Rex Lapis brought his people south of Mt. Tianheng, to found what is now known as Liyue Harbor. They tried to expand their city to Guili Plains, but after Guizhong died during the Archon War, they moved back to Liyue Harbor.

  • Mt. Aocang

    A subarea located in Minlin, Liyue.It was the domain of the adeptus Cloud Retainer while she lived in isolation, following the Archon War and until the events of A Thousand Moonlit Miles.

  • Wangshu Inn

    A subarea located in Bishui Plain, Liyue.Built tall to provide visibility and is a strategic location that must be defended.

  • Feiyun Slope

    A point of interest located in Liyue Harbor, Sea of Clouds, Liyue.It is Liyue Harbor's prosperous merchant district, and is the entrance to the city when coming from the main road to the north. It is centrally located between the two other districts of the Harbor: Chihu Rock to the southeast and Yujing Terrace to the west.

  • Chihu Rock

    It is Liyue Harbor's lower-class district, located on an island to the southeast of the rest of the city. It is connected via bridges to Feiyun Slope to the north and Mt. Tianheng to the west.
