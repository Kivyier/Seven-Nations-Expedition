Featured stops

  • Stadium of the Sacred Flame

    It is considered the core landmark of Natlan, and houses a continually burning fire called the Sacred Flame.

  • People of the Springs basin

    Home to the People of the Springs tribe, this area features vast, gorgeous tropical lagoons and natural geothermal hot springs.

  • Children of the Echoes Canyon

    A beautiful but treacherous quarry area inside the Tequemecan Valley where the Children of Echoes tribe mine for valuable gemstones alongside their Tepetlisaurus companions.

  • Ochkanatlan

    An epic, ancient city ruin suspended high near the sky. Its unique layout and towering architecture make it one of Natlan's most striking visual locations.

  • Ancestral Temple

    It is a sacred site where the people of Natlan once communicated with the Night Kingdom. In modern times, this temple is no longer in use, as the tribes have established a more stable connection with the Night Kingdom. However, it is still considered "holy ground", and is actively guarded by the Scions of the Canopy. According to Legba, only "experienced Couriers" who have passed three trials may enter.
