Featured stops

  • Court of Fontaine

    A subarea and a main city located in Court of Fontaine Region, Fontaine. It was once the court of Focalors, the God of Justice, established at least a thousand years ago. After the events of Masquerade of the Guilty, the court is now under the purview of Chief Justice Neuvillette.

  • Opera Epiclese

    It is an opera house designed to function as the main courthouse of Fontaine, located on the island of Erinnyes. Besides trials, the building hosts a variety of other performances, such as Lyney's magic shows.

  • Lucine Fountain

    A point of interest located in Court of Fontaine Region, Fontaine. It is a fountain situated in front of Opera Epiclese. It is said that all water ultimately gathers at the fountain.

  • Merusea Village

    It is the home of the Melusines. It is located underwater within the body of Elynas and is described as "an adorable and mysterious village where even the chimneys bubble."

  • Annapausis

    It is unlocked during the World Quest The Narzissenkreuz Adventure in Series Ann of the Narzissenkreuz: Act I - The Narzissenkreuz Adventure, and is not labeled on the map prior to this.
