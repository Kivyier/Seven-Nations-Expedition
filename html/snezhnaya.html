Featured stops

  • Zapolyarny Palace outskirts

    It is located in Snezhnograd and is the residence of the Cryo Archon from which they rule over Snezhnaya.

  • Frost-bound Valleys

    The biting, ice-locked terrain that serves as a core entry point to the nation's permafrost zones.

  • Glacial Outposts

    A treacherous high-altitude mountain peak that challenges even the most seasoned adventurers.

  • Svetloledovka Mines

    A vital industrial mining site prone to intense environmental shifts and home to underground secrets.

  • Frostbearing Tree

    It is a new sprout of an ancient Irminsul Tree that was destroyed by the Skyfrost Nail. Revived by the blood of the Abyssal dragon Durin, it finally bloomed once more after the Traveler shattered the Ancient Rime that encased it during the World Quest In the Mountains.
